Local Neural TTS

Runs Kokoro-82M entirely in your browser via kokoro-js. First run downloads ~300 MB; afterwards it's cached and offline.
Click Load model to begin. Best in Chrome/Edge with WebGPU enabled.
  1. Load model — click once. Downloads ~300 MB on first run, then cached offline.
  2. Pick a voice — choose from ~50 voices across 10 languages in the Voice dropdown.
  3. Enter text — type in the box, or drag & drop a .txt file onto it.
  4. Speak — click Speak or press Ctrl+Enter. Long texts are chunked automatically.
  5. Adjust playback — the Playback speed slider changes how fast the audio plays, without regenerating.
  6. Export — save as WAV (lossless) or MP3 (192 kbps, encoded in your browser).
  7. History — last 5 clips appear in a Recent clips card below; each has its own player and download.

Everything runs locally. No audio or text is ever sent to a server.

What this is: A static HTML wrapper around the kokoro-js library, which runs the Kokoro-82M neural TTS model directly in your browser.

Privacy: Audio is generated locally. Your text and the audio are not sent to any server. The only network activity is the one-time download of model weights from the HuggingFace CDN.

First run: Downloads the ONNX model + tokenizer (~300 MB) from https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX. Subsequent runs use the cached copy.

Requirements: Modern Chromium browser. WebGPU: Chrome/Edge 113+. Falls back to WebAssembly if WebGPU is unavailable.

MP3 export: Performed locally in your browser by the bundled lamejs library (file lamejs.iife.js in this folder). No network call. 192 kbps stereo.

Keyboard shortcut: Ctrl+Enter (or Cmd+Enter) to Speak.
